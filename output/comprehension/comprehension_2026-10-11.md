#### Hyperscalers and DOE chase new capacity to feed AI power demand
*101 items · 2 new today · tracked since 2026-06-24*

**Two new models for skipping interconnect queues: bundled onsite generation and solar co-location**

Beyond today's raw capacity additions, two structural approaches emerged for dodging grid bottlenecks: Wärtsilä/Schneider/Stanley Consultants' 'generator-to-chip' bundle integrates onsite generation with electrical build-out as a single delivery package, while Rune Energy is siting modular data centers directly at stranded/excess solar sites rather than waiting for interconnection.

**Why it matters:** Both moves treat the interconnection queue itself as the bottleneck to engineer around rather than wait out — one by vertically bundling generation into the construction contract, the other by chasing power that's already generated but can't reach the grid. For M4, this reinforces that power delivery speed, not just capacity volume, is becoming the competitive axis hyperscalers optimize on.

#### AI backlash organizes into politics and policy
*158 items · 1 new today · tracked since 2026-06-20*

**Liability-dodge framing gets a direct press rebuttal**

After weeks of regulatory and cultural pressure building, an NYT opinion piece directly targets AI companies' legal argument that they shouldn't be liable for their products' unpredictable behavior, arguing existing law should still apply.

**Why it matters:** This is the legal flashpoint underneath the broader backlash: if courts accept that model unpredictability excuses liability, it reshapes product-liability law for all software, not just AI.
