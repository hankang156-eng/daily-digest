Hyperscalers and DOE chase new capacity to feed AI power demand

101 items · 2 new today · tracked since 2026-06-24

Two new models for skipping interconnect queues: bundled onsite generation and solar co-location

Beyond today's raw capacity additions, two structural approaches emerged for dodging grid bottlenecks: Wärtsilä/Schneider/Stanley Consultants' 'generator-to-chip' bundle integrates onsite generation with electrical build-out as a single delivery package, while Rune Energy is siting modular data centers directly at stranded/excess solar sites rather than waiting for interconnection.

Why it mattersBoth moves treat the interconnection queue itself as the bottleneck to engineer around rather than wait out — one by vertically bundling generation into the construction contract, the other by chasing power that's already generated but can't reach the grid. For M4, this reinforces that power delivery speed, not just capacity volume, is becoming the competitive axis hyperscalers optimize on.
