#### AI models claim to crack unsolved math problems
*37 items · 4 new today · tracked since 2026-09-09*

**Claude-assisted Riemann Hypothesis proof claim sparks parallel 'competing proof' chaos**

Mathematician Levent Alpöge announced a Claude-assisted proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, a dramatically bigger claim than the black-hole or Navier-Stokes items that preceded it. Within the same news cycle a second, independent GRH proof claim surfaced from Alessandro Masullo, and HN discussion is now entangling both with the earlier Navier-Stokes claim as if AI may be cracking multiple Millennium-adjacent problems at once.

**Why it matters:** The proof is reportedly 15 million lines long — effectively unverifiable by any single human, which is the crux of the dispute: formal (Lean-style) checkability is being treated as a substitute for human comprehension, and the community isn't settled on whether that's legitimate 'proof.' Two competing claims landing almost simultaneously also raises a credit/priority problem unique to AI-assisted math: if the tool is shared infrastructure, who actually gets to claim the result.
