#### Cheaper AI compute alternatives gain traction
*124 items · 2 new today · tracked since 2026-07-04*

**Z.ai's GLM-5.4 debuts a looped-transformer architecture as a new cheap/open contender**

Following Microsoft's MAI Code 2 and the Inkling-2-Mini debates, Chinese lab Z.ai released GLM-5.4, which reuses weight-shared layers iteratively rather than running each layer once — effectively adding depth without adding parameters.

**Why it matters:** Looped transformers are a real architectural bet for price/performance: more reasoning depth per parameter could mean cheaper inference at similar capability, which directly feeds the alternative-to-Nvidia-scale-compute narrative. HN's reaction mixed technical interest with the recurring China-lab-transparency debate — worth watching whether this architecture gets replicated by Western labs or stays a Z.ai-specific bet.
