# Reader profile

Template for `config/reader_profile.md` — copy this to that filename and fill it in.
The real file is gitignored because it carries internal company framing and this
repo publishes to GitHub Pages.

This is the comprehension layer's *only* source of reader context. It never reads
any external vault or notes system.

**Keep it stable.** No dates, deadlines, build counts, headcount, or current
priorities: those rot, and a stale profile produces confidently wrong framing.
Stable facts, durable interests, and a watchlist only.

## Role

Who you are and what you own, in two or three sentences. Include what you are
*not* — "not an electrical engineer" tells the tool where to stop assuming
fluency, which is as useful as what you do know.

## What the company sells

A stable paragraph a newcomer could read to understand the business. Then one
sentence on the commercial opening — the thing that makes the market exist —
because most of what matters downstream follows from it.

## Why this tool exists

State the job plainly. If the goal is fluency rather than task management, say so:
it changes how everything gets written.

## Audiences that put you on the spot

Who asks you hard questions, and what each expects you to already know. This is
what calibrates the altitude of every explanation.

## Solid on

What to skip as already-known. Be honest — overstating this makes the tool talk
past you.

## Hazy on

Where explanations should start from first principles. This is the highest-value
section and the one most worth revisiting; it is also the one the reading marks
(`knew this` / `over my head`) will refine over time.

## Entity watchlist

Names worth noticing in the news, grouped. Include competitors and adjacent
players, not just your own counterparties — their moves change the landscape.
Suggested groupings: incumbents; direct comparables; suppliers; customers and
platforms; standards bodies and labs; channel partners.

## Guardrails

Anything the tool's output must respect. For example: this page is internal;
nothing derived from it goes into external material without review; specific
terms that must not be used externally; specific circulating figures that are
unsourced and must not be repeated as fact.
